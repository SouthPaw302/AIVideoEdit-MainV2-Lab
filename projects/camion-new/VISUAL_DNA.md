# Visual DNA — Mountain Noir en español

Nocturnal road film built from moisture on glass, wet asphalt, mountain distance, a solitary cab, empty-seat memory, warm practical lights, and a believable drift toward dawn. Camera movement is slow when the lyric needs room and more directional during the sustained road passages. FX remain visible but motivated: rain/reflection continuity, fog and pigment travel, parallax, rack focus, halation, memory echoes, and authored transitions.

Protected: faces and hands, truck geometry, horizon continuity, lyric readability, and the emotional restraint of the song. Avoid repetitive rain-only treatment, arbitrary zooms, heavy glitches, false geography, and unmotivated spectacle.
