# Visual DNA — Proposal, NOT CANON

**User direction:** Miami realism. Naturalistic photography-like night scenes, plausible working harbor geography, worn dock timber, lantern light, salt air, low warm tavern lamps, open water, boats, palm trees and distant Miami skyline. A single dancing couple: woman in red-and-black ruffled dress, man in dark hat and vest. Musicians: acoustic guitarist, violin/fiddle, palmas and hand percussion, with rap interlude. Transition from deep blue midnight/amber to orange sunrise. All subjects and scene geography must remain consistent across shots. Avoid tourist-video clichés, fake labels, fabricated geographic claims or visual gimmicks. Do not add surreal motion.

**Current test limitations:** imagery comes from model-generated composites/crops, not authentic photography, has repeated angles, and no independently rendered human articulation. No baseline acceptance or locked geometry yet.
