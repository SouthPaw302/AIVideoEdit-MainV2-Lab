## Reusable methods observed

1. **Keep shots alive before cutting.** The [El Viento Route 4 checkpoint](https://github.com/SouthPaw302/AIVideoEdit/blob/song/el-viento-trae-tu-nombre/projects/el-viento-trae-tu-nombre/refinements/route4-two-heroes/CHECKPOINT_CURRENT.json) explicitly favors fewer hard replacements, longer hero holds, transparent/variant assets, rain/light/thread/material motion, foreground depth and restrained camera movement. Its source motion archives contain clean loops, GIF previews and pre-FX motion sequences, some backed up as Batch 07–09 ZIPs.
2. **Build a per-shot source family, not a single video generator dependency.** [El Viento FX variants](https://github.com/SouthPaw302/AIVideoEdit/blob/song/el-viento-trae-tu-nombre/projects/el-viento-trae-tu-nombre/FX_VARIANTS_MANIFEST.json) are alternate bounded still states for interpolation, morphing and compositing; [Route 4 Batch 01 shot locks](https://github.com/SouthPaw302/AIVideoEdit/blob/song/el-viento-trae-tu-nombre/projects/el-viento-trae-tu-nombre/refinements/route4-two-heroes/shot_locks/BATCH_01_OPENING_REMEMBRANCE.json) tie protected geography, identity, shot motion, music cues and FX IDs to each moving proof. Preserve clean vs processed variants and source SHAs.
3. **Batch gates and durable storage.** [Tribal House Batch Execution](https://github.com/SouthPaw302/AIVideoEdit/blob/song/tribal-house-remastered/projects/tribal-house-remastered/BATCH_EXECUTION_SCRIPT.md) defines the method `10 main images → hash/archive → small FX gate → 10 derivative loop/GIF/motion/FX states → archive → QC/lock → next batch`. Source-first, internal-world motion before camera; GitHub stores recipes, proofs/hashes and state; heavy media goes to Drive.
4. **Meaningful localized motion.** [Silver Coin FX methods](https://github.com/SouthPaw302/AIVideoEdit/blob/song/silver-coin-alternate/projects/silver-coin/FX_METHODS.md) describe preserved pigment texture, depth proxy, planar parallax, micro-warp for cloth/crowd/foliage, advected atmosphere, motivated firelight, reflection ripple and structural match transitions. [Silver Coin camera grammar](https://github.com/SouthPaw302/AIVideoEdit/blob/song/silver-coin-alternate/projects/silver-coin/V6_MUSIC_CAMERA_GRAMMAR.md) uses measured music to direct bounded push/pull/camera accents, not face/hand warping. In that source, 2D Gaussian light fields explicitly **are not** genuine 3DGS.
5. **Shot packages → proof → master finishing.** [Irish Eyes finishing stack](https://github.com/SouthPaw302/AIVideoEdit/blob/song/irish-eyes/projects/irish-eyes/EDITORIAL_FINISHING_STACK.md) requires source/protected subject/masks/alpha/depth/environment/FX/transition packages and a moving proof for each; then full-song assembly and distinct grading/color, optical, stabilization, editorial and actual-export QC. Its [3DGS pipeline](https://github.com/SouthPaw302/AIVideoEdit/blob/song/irish-eyes/projects/irish-eyes/3DGS_PIPELINE.md) requires actual overlapping footage/camera poses/splat reconstruction; SuperSplat is inspection/rendering, not model training.
6. **Do not overfit character motion.** [Leave It by the Door V3 FX](https://github.com/SouthPaw302/AIVideoEdit/blob/song/leave-it-by-the-door/projects/leave-it-by-the-door/V3_FX_MANIFEST.json) uses localized water/weather/fire/lighting with locked/restrained cameras and explicit face/hand/instrument protection. [Nordic shot-proof recipe](https://github.com/SouthPaw302/AIVideoEdit/blob/song/nordic-remastered/projects/nordic-remastered/SHOT_PROOF_RENDER_RECIPE.json) separates living-scene localized warp and cinematic camera movement and does not claim final FX lock from representative proofs alone.
7. **Audit the preserved movie before stacking FX.** [El Viento B01 FX gate](https://github.com/SouthPaw302/AIVideoEdit/blob/song/el-viento-trae-tu-nombre/projects/el-viento-trae-tu-nombre/refinements/route4-two-heroes/fx/BATCH_01_FX_GATE.json) explicitly rejects double-applying already visible light/bloom or replacing a valid locked baseline with older batch clips. FX IDs alone do not prove visual impact; the finished moving edit must be inspected. The reference [FX lock](https://github.com/SouthPaw302/AIVideoEdit/blob/song/el-viento-trae-tu-nombre/.github/workflows/el-viento-fx-lock.yml) runs boot plus real precompile/verification on the song branch.

**Gaussian naming law:** image-space Gaussian glows/splats/blur ≠ reconstructed true 3D Gaussian Splatting. A real scene asset and 3DGS preflight are required before calling any output true Gaussian splatting; otherwise mark it as Gaussian 2D treatment or 2.5D/parallax.

## La Luna-only interpretation authorized by the user

The character/shot-motion compile is a **shot-local hybrid compositing and animation stage**, not automatically a call to an AI video model. Starting with the La Luna canonical clean source still and hash: make approved duplicate/motion-variant states; segment and protect faces, hands, props, wardrobe, and geography; derive clean GIF/MP4 loops; layer materials, depth planes, and transparent assets; use matched canonical FX, Gaussian image-space light where justified, and restrained Director camera/cut directions; align source/local loops to the song's established ONNX musical cues; export an actual evolving moving-shot proof. Preserve original/variant/clean/pre-FX/final-pre-master separately with hashes, receipts and Drive backup.

**Semantic truth remains mandatory:** a simulated background sway or moving camera does not prove live guitar fingering, bowing, castanet playing or articulate dance steps. First use credible source-derived character frames/alternate poses/rig/motion footage when authorized; where the available sources cannot depict the storyboard's required action convincingly, declare that shot `PERFORMANCE_MOTION_GAP` and escalate to a separately authorized bounded continuation rather than manufacturing a false PASS. Shots without people/action can record `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

Master FX/Assembly then selects approved per-shot visual layers and motion performances, establishes continuity and editorial rhythm, aligns ONNX beat and independently verified lyric cues, renders, grades and scans the actual complete encoded export. No new storyboard, other-film visual DNA, unrelated asset reuse, or default wholesale regeneration.
