# Director's treatment — El Camión y la Carretera

**Treatment status: ORIGINAL PRE-PRODUCTION CONCEPT / NOT LOCKED.** No verified complete source WAV or lyric master yet. Based on known title and broad user-supplied song themes, not a pretended final lyric interpretation.

## Story thesis
A long night shift is not merely a drive. The mountain highway compresses what a man carries: loss, absence and an honest need to continue. The truck is not fetishized; it is a real place where solitude has weight. The road never becomes a magic portal. Dawn gives permission to continue, not a cheap happy ending.

## Three arcs
1. **Work and night (shots 01-10):** establish the driver's physical world—engine, rain, highway, roadside light and absence.
2. **Memory and endurance (shots 11-20):** restrained flashes of an empty house, unsent letter and the return to driving; tension lives in real geography and carried objects.
3. **No false ending (shots 21-30):** fog, mountains, stillness and an incomplete sunrise; man and machine continue forward.

## Look and sound
Wide 16:9. Graphite and sodium amber initially, cobalt at 02:00 feeling (not literal timecode), muted warm daylight. Fixed 35/50/85mm lens vocabulary, realistic truck scale and terrain. Real world motion should make shots live; no global pan-and-zoom pretending to be an animated person. Preserve original song WAV sample-for-sample; optional ambience is separable and must not overwrite the score. No on-screen lyric captions by default.
