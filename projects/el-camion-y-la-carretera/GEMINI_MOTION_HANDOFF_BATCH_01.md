## Continuations for genuine movement
Use the corresponding approved original still as the **starting image** and preserve identity, lighting, lens/camera axis and scenery.
- **EC-02 ignition:** Driver's existing hand turns key once; amber dashboard lights emerge. Fixed passenger-side camera, realistic human anatomy, no extra hands, no changing gauges. Do not alter the truck's dashboard layout.
- **EC-03 first mile:** Same truck continues rightward physically on wet road; believable wheel rotation and spray, realistic parallax, no wheelbase/body changes. Road geometry does not morph.
- **EC-04 wiper:** One correctly hinged windshield-wiper sweep clears rain on glass; driver's existing silhouette/hand remains stable, rain stays outside cab.
- **EC-07 gas station:** Truck enters station slowly; preserve same truck design and gas station architecture, no teleporting, no spontaneously moving pumps.
- **EC-10 departure:** Truck moves away along physically continuous dark road, red taillights diminish naturally; no arbitrary camera zoom.
Other shots can use their **canonical MainV2 FX GIF/MP4 loops** for rain, fog, steam and local practical light; real semantic action needs source-continuation.

## Mandatory delivery for each Gemini clip
1. 16:9 MP4, ideally 24 fps native cadence, use its start frame as reference; **do not generate replacement songs or narration**.
2. Include shot ID, source Drive file, original image SHA256, generator/provider version and export fps/duration.
3. Send clip to the project's FX/QC folder for human + Zero-Drift comparison.
4. Mark generated audio `discard` unless explicitly desired as separate ambience.
5. Do not promote terminal frame to next-shot anchor until inspection approves identity, physical truck geometry, atmosphere, no cuts and temporal consistency.
