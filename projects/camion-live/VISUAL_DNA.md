# Visual DNA — Mountain Noir en español, real footage

Real-footage nocturnal road film built from windshield reflections, wet asphalt, mountain distance, lake air, vehicle travel, warm practical lights, and a believable drift toward dawn. The opening and closing breathe: a long title pause before the first road image and a long, quiet end card after the song. Camera movement is slow when the lyric needs room and more directional during sustained road passages. FX remain visible but motivated: weather continuity, fog and pigment travel, subtle parallax, rack focus, halation, memory echoes, color-grade evolution, and authored transitions.

Protected: original source media, faces and hands, vehicle geometry, horizon continuity, lyric readability, and the emotional restraint of the song. Avoid repetitive rain-only treatment, arbitrary zooms, heavy glitches, false geography, Puerto Rico footage, and unmotivated spectacle.
