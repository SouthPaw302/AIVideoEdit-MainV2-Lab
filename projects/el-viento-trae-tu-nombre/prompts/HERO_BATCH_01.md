# Hero Render Batch 01 — 11 Plates

All plates use the locked Visual DNA, 16:9, no readable text, no logos, no modern elements.

1. **Map Awakening:** extreme close heavy aged linen, tactile weave, faded nautical-map ink emerging from fibers, museum-painting quality.
2. **Embroidered Cartography:** antique sea map on linen, jewel-toned embroidery physically threading across coastlines/currents, tactile fibers and pigment.
3. **Ocean Made of Memory:** wide tapestry where map ink and embroidery liquefy into deep blue-green ocean currents while canvas weave remains visible.
4. **Village Rising from the Sea:** thick impasto ocean currents gather into a humble old island village, sea-worn simple houses emerging from paint.
5. **Village Becoming Fort:** medium continuity plate where rustic houses share geometry with weathered historical fort walls, stable horizon/lighting.
6. **The Imagined Homeland:** wide idealized landscape formed from dissolving fort pigment; mountains, shore and luminous painted sky suggested through memory-soft brushwork.
7. **Veil of Memory:** homeland crossed by layered painted smoke, translucent cloud washes and watercolor veils; independent atmospheric strata.
8. **Ancestors in the Mist:** dark featureless silhouettes emerging from and dissolving into smoke, cloud and water; anonymous ancestral presence, elegant not horrific.
9. **The Whole Tapestry:** complete handcrafted tapestry suspended in deep dark void, containing coherent traces of map, embroidery, ocean, village, fort, homeland and silhouettes, corners prepared to fold inward.
10. **One Glowing Thread:** single vibrant luminous textile thread suspended in near-black; tiny internal currents of prior palette moving under its fibers.
11. **The Name Carried Away:** same thread caught by unseen force, bending and stretching into darkness with directional negative space; fiber-like light trail, designed to resolve to black.
