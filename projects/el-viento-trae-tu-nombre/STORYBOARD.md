# Island Memory Tapestry — Locked User Storyboard

## Visual Style
A living, breathing painting. Transitions are fluid and dreamlike: pigment dissolves, structural morphs, flowing textures and organic movement rather than hard cuts. Imagery must feel handcrafted, tactile and continuously transforming, as though memory itself is reshaping the canvas.

## Sequence 1 — A Living Collage
1. **ECU:** aged linen/canvas; antique nautical-map ink draws itself into the fibers. Slow dolly out.
2. **MS:** bright embroidery blooms and threads through the established map. Static camera; internal motion only.
3. **WS:** map and embroidery liquefy into deep blue/green ocean currents while retaining painted-fabric tactility. Slow graceful pan.

## Sequence 2 — Transforming Architecture
4. **CU:** thick ocean paint rises into a small rustic island village. Slow dolly in.
5. **MS:** village hardens, weathers and morphs into imposing historical fort walls. Slow bounded orbit with uninterrupted spatial continuity.
6. **WS:** fort dissolves into luminous pigment and reforms as an abstract imagined homeland. Gradual upward tilt toward painted sky.

## Sequence 3 — The Ethereal Presence
7. **MS:** painted smoke, translucent cloud and watercolor textures cross and obscure the homeland. Static composition; atmosphere provides motion.
8. **CU:** dark featureless human silhouettes form, fade and reappear within smoke/cloud/water. Slow pan; no faces or identities.

## Sequence 4 — The Power of the Name
9. **WS:** accelerating pullback reveals the entire tapestry in a dark void; all four corners fold inward, collapsing all accumulated imagery toward the center.
10. **CU:** tapestry compresses into one vibrant glowing thread; earlier colors/textures travel within it. Static camera; subtle internal vibration/light.
11. **ECU:** unseen force catches the thread; it bends, stretches and is swept away. One fluid camera follow until the thread vanishes. End on pure black.
