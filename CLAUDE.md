# AIVideoEdit
**First command of every new Claude session:** `python bootstrap.py boot --repo-root <repo>`.

Do not begin production by reading/copying rules manually.

After bootstrap PASS, read in this order:
1. `.aivideoedit/os/PRIME_DIRECTIVE.md`
2. `.aivideoedit/SECOND_BRAIN.md`
3. `.aivideoedit/os/SOUL.md`

For Director Brain v2 projects, do not begin production until `SECOND_BRAIN.md` shows the direction authority, production mode, canon, accepted baseline, refinement scope, forbidden changes, and exact next action. Claude-specific instructions never override the AIVideoEdit OS.
